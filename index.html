🛠️ What You Can Do With YuE-Studio

Let's take a closer look at the main capabilities. Even if you're brand new to music software, you'll find YuE-Studio intuitive and forgiving.

🎹 Generate Music with AI (YuE2)

Describe the kind of music you want in plain language — "upbeat synth-pop with a driving bassline and cheerful vocals" — and YuE-Studio will create a complete, high-quality audio track for you. The YuE2 engine runs locally on your Mac, ensuring your creativity stays private and your generation is fast.

✂️ Edit Your Audio Like a Pro

Every edit you make in YuE-Studio is reversible. That means you can experiment freely: cut a verse, add reverb, change the tempo, or reorder entire sections — and if you don't like the result, simply undo it. No permanent changes, no "saved over the original" regrets.

🎛️ Master Your Tracks to Perfection

Mastering is the final polish that makes a track sound professional and consistent. YuE-Studio includes a suite of mastering tools — EQ, compression, stereo enhancement, loudness normalization, and more — all designed to be simple enough for beginners but powerful enough for pros. You'll get your tracks sounding radio-ready in minutes.

📦 Export in the Format You Need

When you're happy with your track, export it as a lossless file (WAV or FLAC) for maximum quality, or as an MP3 for easy sharing and smaller file sizes. YuE-Studio handles both perfectly.

💻 System Requirements

  • Hardware: Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M1 Pro/Max/Ultra, M2, M2 Pro/Max/Ultra, M3, M3 Pro/Max/Ultra, or newer)
  • Operating System: macOS 13 (Ventura) or later (macOS 14 Sonoma recommended)
  • Memory: 8GB RAM minimum (16GB or more recommended for larger projects)
  • Storage: 1GB of free space for the app, plus additional space for your audio projects
  • Internet: Not required for everyday use — everything runs locally. Only needed for downloading the app and future updates.
💡 Note: YuE-Studio is specifically built for Apple Silicon Macs. It will not run on Intel-based Macs or Windows PCs. This is intentional — it allows us to deliver the best possible performance and efficiency on the latest hardware.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is YuE-Studio really free?

Yes! YuE-Studio is completely free and open-source. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no in-app purchases. It's a community-driven project, and we welcome contributions and feedback.

Do I need any coding or technical skills?

Absolutely not. YuE-Studio is designed for everyone. The interface is clean, visual, and intuitive. If you can use a standard word processor, you can use YuE-Studio.

Can I use YuE-Studio commercially?

Yes. Any music you create with YuE-Studio is yours. You can use it for personal projects, release it commercially, or anything in between — no royalties, no attribution required.

What if I run into a problem?

Check the GitHub repository's Issues section for known problems and solutions. You can also open a new issue, and the community will do its best to help you.

📖 Tips for Your First Project

  1. Start simple: Try generating a short loop or a simple chord progression first to get a feel for the tools.
  2. Experiment with undo: Don't be afraid to try radical edits. Since everything is reversible, you have nothing to lose.
  3. Use the mastering presets: If you're new to mastering, start with a preset and adjust from there. You'll quickly learn what each knob does.
  4. Save often: While the app is stable, it's always good practice to save your project files regularly.
